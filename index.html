I. Institutional Mandate

The Mysuru Forum for Diplomatic & Archival Studies (MFDAS) is an autonomous, student-convened academic circle and reference depository based in Mysuru, Karnataka. The Forum is organized exclusively for educational, non-partisan, and non-commercial research into primary-source state publications, comparative public administration, and diplomatic history across Eurasia and the Indian Ocean littoral.

II. Working Research Modules (2026–2027)

  • Module I — East Asian Public Administration: Comparative single-payer healthcare architectures, National Health Insurance data integration, and classical textual preservation.
  • Module II — Non-Aligned Statecraft & Reconstruction: Archival documentation of Afro-Asian bilateral treaties, NAM communiqués, and socialist post-war reconstruction frameworks.
  • Module III — Princely State Civic Engineering: Administrative modernism under the Wadiyar dynasty (1881–1947), irrigation infrastructure, and urban botanical planning in the Cauvery basin.
  • Module IV — Trans-Eurasian Corridors: Silk Road diplomatic manuscripts, Central Asian archival treaties, and Indian Ocean maritime security trade routes.
